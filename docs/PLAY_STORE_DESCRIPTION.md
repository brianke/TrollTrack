# TrollTrack — Google Play listing text

## Short description

Log trolling trips, catches, GPS locations, gear setups, and conditions offline.

## Full description

TrollTrack is a simple fishing log built to help you capture what matters on the water: where you were, what you were running, what the conditions were, and what you caught. It’s designed for quick entry while you’re fishing and for easy review later when you want to spot patterns that lead to better days on the water.

Use TrollTrack to start a trip and record catches as you fish. Each catch is saved with a timestamp and can be reviewed later in your trip history. The Dashboard shows your current position and recent trips so you can quickly get back to the information you care about without digging through menus.

TrollTrack can record catch locations using your device’s GPS and display coordinates in formats that are easy to read and reference. It also supports map features through Google Maps so you can visualize where activity happened and return to productive water.

Weather conditions are shown alongside your fishing activity so you can connect catches with temperature and other conditions over time. If you choose to configure a weather API key, TrollTrack can retrieve current conditions to help you document what the lake was doing when you were getting bites.

You can also build a personal gear library, including lures with details like manufacturer, description, buoyancy, length, and weight. Adding photos helps you identify gear quickly, and your saved gear can be used when setting up and logging.

TrollTrack stores your data locally on your device and does not require an account to use. Location access is used to record fishing positions and support mapping, camera and photo access are used when you attach images, and network access is used for map tiles and optional weather lookups.

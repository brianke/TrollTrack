1. Summary

TrollTrack is a fishing log and trip companion. Most information you enter or generate is stored on your device. We do not operate our own account system or cloud sync in the app as described here; data stays local unless you choose to share it outside the app yourself. The app uses Google Maps for mapping and may call WeatherAPI.com when you configure a weather API key, which sends approximate location to that service for forecasts.

2. Information the app processes

  • Fishing and trip data you enter, such as catches, trips, species, gear (lures, divers), line-out, notes, and related details.
  • Location data from your device’s GPS or network location (latitude, longitude, and related metadata such as accuracy, altitude, speed, course, and timestamps) when you allow location access. This is used to associate catches and trips with where you were and to show maps. Location may be stored in the app’s local database.
  • Photos you take or select (for example lure or catch images), stored on your device as part of app data.
  • Settings such as an optional WeatherAPI key you save in the app (stored locally on your device).
  • Technical / connectivity: the app uses the internet for map tiles and, when configured, weather requests. Standard network operations may involve IP addresses and device information as handled by those services under their policies.

3. Local storage on your device

TrollTrack stores your data primarily in a local database on your phone or tablet. You control that device. Uninstalling the app may remove local data depending on your operating system and backup settings. Android may allow backup of app data if enabled on your device (see your device backup settings).

4. Permissions

The app may request permissions such as:

  • Location (precise and/or approximate) — to record and display where you fish and to support mapping and weather features.
  • Camera — to take photos you attach in the app.
  • Photos / storage — to save or pick images, depending on Android version.
  • Network — to load maps and weather data.

You can revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may stop working if you do.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Maps / Google Play services — Map display and related functionality use Google services. Google’s handling of data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Map usage may send requests (for example map tile and location-related requests) to Google as part of providing maps.
  • WeatherAPI.com — If you add a WeatherAPI key in the app, the app requests weather information from WeatherAPI.com. Those requests typically include location coordinates needed for a forecast. See WeatherAPI’s privacy information. We do not control WeatherAPI’s servers; your use is subject to their terms and privacy policy.

6. Analytics and advertising

This app does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking analytics as part of the TrollTrack codebase described in this policy. If you add such services in the future, update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section accordingly.

7. Children’s privacy

TrollTrack is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently received information from a child, contact us at the email above.

8. International users

If you use the app outside the country where the developer resides, your information may be processed on your device and by third parties (such as Google or WeatherAPI) in various countries, according to their policies and applicable law.

9. Your choices and data deletion

Because data is stored locally, you can remove much of it by deleting entries inside the app or by uninstalling the app. For requests related to this policy or questions about data practices, email us at the contact address above.

10. Security

We design the app to keep your fishing data on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; use the app at your own risk and protect access to your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy, except where your local law requires a different process.
